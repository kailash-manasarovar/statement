**For thirty years, British women have been disappearing and turning up dead around the Spanish coastal town of Denia. Nobody investigates. Nobody wants to know.**

Until one woman refused to die.

They poisoned her again and again, then followed her across the world, terrorizing her as she tried to find safety.

Distracting her endlessly with the promise of a police investigation, they performed sedated surgery on her, stealing her eggs, a secret she was never supposed to discover.

She survived and she found out.

What happens when a beautiful Mediterranean beach resort becomes a hunting ground for tourists and foreigners? Who protects the men who prey on women and children and babies? Why do foreign criminals set up shop there because the law will cease to apply to them once they arrive?

And what happened to the women who disappeared?

The deeper she digs, the uglier it gets. Corruption. Violence. Pornography. Predators. Criminal networks hiding behind respectable memes. And a trail that leads back thirty years to one tawdry act of male violence on a much-loved woman.

Then there are the **salmon mousses**: a mysterious group running an intuitive programme, a programme that sent our heroine into hell in 2006.

And the **lupins of Denia** — who are the strange, protected force at the centre of it all.

This isn't a case that can be solved by following the rules.

The people who wrote the rules are behind the murders.

And the woman they tried to kill may be the only witness who can tell the world what really happened.

**They thought she was a victim.**

**They were wrong.**
