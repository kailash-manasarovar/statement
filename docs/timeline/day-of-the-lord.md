# Day Of The Lord

The Day Of The Lord is a seven year period related to the end times and the return of Lord Jesus Christ as mentioned in the Bible.

It's not clear when exactly this period began; whether it was at the setting up of the Abomination of Desolation in December 2021, or when I began Jesus's teachings from the workbook of A Course In Miracles in February 2022.

Or some other time...

We're not yet out, that's for sure.
