## Sullivan

- It's January and we've just buried my aunt Geraldine.
- Geraldine worked for Pan Am and had been called out at the last minute to work over Christmas 1988.
- She was the purser on Pan Am 103 which was bombed over Lockerbie.
- My mum invites everyone back to our house after the funeral; feeds and waters them.
- There are *hundreds* of people in our house that day.
- I'm upstairs hiding from it all. It's intense.
- Downstairs, dad meets a man called Sullivan.
- Sullivan works for Pan Am in a management role of some sort and knew Geraldine well.
- Dad realizes he is the colleague Geraldine had told him about in the past; the one that brings the thick black briefcase onto the plane at Heathrow.
- Geraldine takes the bag and keeps it in the purser's office and the bag is picked up in New York.
- My dad remembers what his sister had told him about the thick black bag - perhaps it's common airline practice - as he's talking with this man Sullivan in our dining room.
- Or perhaps he remembered the details after meeting the man because the more significant thing for him was how the man had made him feel.
- Damien said he *felt* an intense negative energy emanating from the man Sullivan; in his mannerisms, the patterns of his voice, and it wasn't like the grief the other Pan Am staff were experiencing and expressing.
- It was unusual, he said.
- He may have said he felt the man was suffering from overwhelming guilt (and my dad is familiar enough with this emotion to recognize it in another person).
- My dad became firmly convinced that the man Sullivan had been involved in the bombing. 
- The feeling he got from him, and then remembering what Geraldine had told him regarding the bag made him certain.
- Dad told this story again and again, to everyone, loudly.
- He still does if you ask him about it. He hasn't changed his story.
- Whenever dad tells anyone about this, he starts off with the energy-emanating part of the story before retelling what Geraldine had told him about the bag, as if this had always been the bigger, more obvious thing in his mind.
- He told this story to so many people without realizing the Americans were listening very carefully to everything going on at 31, and still are.

### Hillsborough

- Pan Am 103 was supposed to have gone down in the Atlantic like the Air India jet a few years before, all evidence disappearing into the sea.
- Instead, due to a late departure, evidence fell onto over 2,000 square kilometers of the Scottish countryside, bodies littering people's gardens and hanging from their smashed up roofs.
- It was an absolute disaster and everyone was asking questions.
- The Americans felt the need to silence the constant media interest.
- They hatched probably the most evil distraction plan anyone has ever come up with; the Hillsborough disaster.
- They relied on horses.[^1]
- Strangely, I was watching the match that day.
- I don't like football very much so I'm not sure why I was watching it.
- After a very short time, the game was stopped by the referee and I watched those poor people dying in the stands.
- Liverpudlians were not convinced this was an accident - especially the criminal gangs - and many people were paid off by the Americans for their silence.
- I heard that even the referee got a pay off.
- Thanks to the Americans, Lockerbie and Hillsborough gave criminals massive leverage over the British police, and government too I expect.
- And this expanded into something of a *free-for-all* when it came to sex offending and porn production.

### Silencing Damien

- The Americans continued to be concerned about my dad's meeting with Sullivan, and his inability to shut up about it, so they hatched a silencing plan for him too.
- Their plan was to get sex-tapes of his daughter (me) and threaten him with them.
- With help from my friends' families and associates, they organized Tottenham rape gangs (who may have been sex-"industry" helpers at the time rather than what we know now as *rape-gangs*) - headed by Winston May and Nicky the Soho prostitute - to target me during the summer holidays.
- They even got me set up with a summer job at H Samuels in Brent Cross where I was working with criminal porn-gang members and their helpers.
- My schoolfriend Geetha, her older brother Arul, and her mum Sonia Singham can shed light on how this was all arranged.
- What the Americans could never have predicted is how my other friends (Willow, Cathy, and Sara - innocent civilian bystanders yet again) were also targeted for sedated rape-porn because it is so easy to do and we never know it's happened!
- And, of course, because of the American protection.
- It's likely my friends became as famous in porn as me at the time; and could even have had porn-babies organized and paid for by wealthy perverts when that sick *genre* took off.
- Geetha was aware of the sex-frenzy getting out of control - it was only supposed to be me! - and made some obvious efforts with her family to ensure we thought she was included too; a situation set up with an African boy Emmanuel whose friends apparently robbed her of a ring, but it was bogus. 
- The woman hated me so much while I was suffering from severe PTSD from multiple rapes, and never spoke to me unless it was to insult me, but was overly vocal about *all-these-things-that-had-happened-she-needed-me-to-know-about*... didn't make any sense to me at the time, does now.
- I was listening quietly as usual.
- You can read all about what happened to me in 1989 at the hands of the Tottenham rape gangs in two statements I gave to the Metropolitan Police; a video interview given in January 2006 when I remembered a single instance of gang rape, and another extensive report and video from 2015.
- Once the Americans had copies of the gang-rape porn films I starred in sedated with Winston May and his friends in their bedrooms, and their mothers' kitchens, and on their mothers' dining room tables, they showed it to Damien to shut him up about Sullivan.
- I suspect he will have been shown these films through connected British government, civil servants and spies like Fraser, Kevin Joice, and others.
- But, Damien, my dad, is a sexual deviant and predator and instead of being horrified at what they had done to his 16-year-old daughter, he found the films so delightful, he promised he'd keep quiet if they made more.
- And at this point, he starts blaming Israel for Lockerbie but never changes his story about Sullivan whenever he's asked about it.
- Soon after, I was introduced to Chris Ludwick and the N8 band, also through my friends Geetha and Willow.
- And the rest is history.
- All this snowballed into something so monstrous whereby men watching the resulting porn of police-and-government-silenced targets became aware they could do whatever they liked to women and children, then babies as their tastes evolved, and the women and children would never know and if they did find out it wouldn't matter anyway.
- Anarchy essentially, protected at the highest level.
- I believe this is what fueled the grooming gang epidemic also; American silencing due to Lockerbie and Hillsborough, which all the porn-addicts rather liked anyway.
- All this robbed me - and arguably Willow, Cathy, and Sara too - of our human rights because if we ever found out what had been done to us, the police would have been forced to ignore us due to the CIA gagging orders.
- Back in the day, this might have bothered the police; but very quickly they found this marvelous! And you just have to look at the rape-gang stories to see how complicit the British police have been in the mass rape of British women.
- So one mess to clean up another to clean up another, and all they've got is a giant and unsupportable mess which is bound to collapse, and should have collapsed except for high-level porn-addict collusion.
- Lockerbie itself, I heard, was an attempt to fix the problem of rogue spies out of control... and the irony is they made a world-destroying, rogue-spy movement instead.

### Geraldine's anxiety

- I visited Tundergarth Church in 2019.
- It's opposite the field where the cockpit fell.
- There's a small building next to the church with a memory book where people have written things about their friends and family that were murdered that night.
- I looked up my aunt Geraldine Murphy and found that someone had written something about her.
- A colleague from Pan Am had said a few words, and interestingly mentioned that Geraldine had been constantly anxious throughout her life.
- I could have been reading about myself.
