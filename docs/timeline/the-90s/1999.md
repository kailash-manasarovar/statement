## Mallorca

- Shortly after this, I decide I'm going on holiday on my own to Mallorca.
- I feel like I've lost all my friends.
- In Mallorca, I meet a Dutchman, Patrick Evers from Alkmaar who is hanging out with a Scottish man, and I decide I'm in love with him (I'm not) and move to Holland.
- I think Patrick and the Scottish man set up at least one "event" in Mallorca.
- I'm told all this was set up by North London's finest, and I do not doubt it for a second.
- I leave him about three months after moving to Holland and end up living in Amsterdam and working for Philips Electronics as a webmaster, which I really loved and made me interested in computer science again, since school and having to put all my energy into just existing with severe and constant PTSD symptoms.
