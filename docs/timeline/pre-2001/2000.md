## Blood poisoning in Alkmaar

- Patrick and I do not get on.
- When his mum picks me up in the car from the airport she tells me, *I give it 3 months*.
- He's horrible.
- He's charging me 300 guilder for the rent and it turns out the council is charging his sister only 200 guilders.
- His sister is living with a really dodgy bloke who comes round with his mates sometimes and I get a bad feeling from them.
- I start looking for somewhere else to live pretty much immediately.
- I get a job quick though, a good one, with Philips in Amsterdam.
- There was an incident when I went to look at a flat in Amsterdam and it was obviously a hooker's joint; the Russian woman who met me was driving a very expensive red sports car, it was all very dodgy... 
- Anyway, I get my wisdom tooth out, and it doesn't heal properly and I'm unwell.
- Patrick - probably for the first moment ever - is extremely kind to me and says he'll make me some soup.
- So he does.
- From then on I become extremely ill, not just a little bit, and I end up in the hospital with blood poisoning and leave Alkmaar and move into a colleague's attic in the Rivierenbuurt with no toilet or shower - just a tap - but it's home!

## Queen's Day

- Roseanne Pompeus (Paul and Matt's sister) and Simon Collins come over to Amsterdam for Queen's Day.
- I have not long got out of hospital and my immune system does not exist because of the antibiotics I was given intravenously.
- They visit me at my studio flat and we drink a lot of beer.
- My tolerance for alcohol is super low.
- Next we go and see Roseanne's friend Deak who is visiting his mates who live in Amsterdam too.
- Now, Deak is friends with Roseanne through Martine, and Martine is the woman who dropped out of going to Benidorm at the last minute in 1997 - the PA to the Beatles man in Abbey Road.
- The woman who, had she gone, would have ended up in Dénia.
- I wonder if she was warned not to go because of what had just happened.
- Anyway.
- Simon and Roseanne leave me with Deak and his friends and we go back to theirs.
- One of Deak's friends is Barry White, who, incidentally, worked for Goldman's while I was there in 2018 - I didn't meet him but saw his profile pic on the intranet and knew it was him.
- I get extremely drunk and they give me a tiny bit of cocaine and I make an utter idiot of myself and feel like I want the ground to swallow me up the next day - I don't think I have *EVER* made that much of an idiot of myself!
- So I'm feeling very sorry for myself and I eventually go to meet everyone the next day in a bar in the Oudekerk area. 
- I'm very quiet and embarrassed, and unwell too from having drunk too much.
- And the woman that was with them all, who I never met or said hello to because I was dying with embarrassment, a woman I believe I only ever met once (I might have met her the night before briefly too), I saw the other day at the thermal springs in Cauterets.
- I *knew* I recognized you. So nice to see you again - sorry for my dreadful behavior in Holland and unfriendliness at the time. Yeah, was your name Bianca? I've no recollection but it sounds a bit right.
