# 1975

## A dream

- I'm on summer holiday with my mum, and dad maybe too if he was still coming, in Northern Ireland at Granny and Granda's house on the Craigetempin Road near Ballymoney; they might have knocked the house down now I'm not sure.
- I'm two
- I'm sleeping in the upstairs room at the front, in the double bed with granny.
- I have a dream which feels real, as if I'm not sleeping.
- There's a train track not very far away from the house, a few hundred metres, and you can hear the trains going along day and night, so I had been hearing this all the time and it wasn't unusual.
- I hear the train, but it does something different; it turns and starts to come towards the house, and it's coming up the field, then up the garden path, and it's a massive old big black steam train, and it's getting closer and closer, and I'm frightened now, and it comes through the window and all the windows smash as it does.
- And I wake up traumatized.
- And I never forget it.

### The train

- I dream of that same train a lot over the years, it's a recurring dream.
- Often, I'm at the platform and it's the end of the line, and the train has arrived, and there's big warehouse sliding doors, or like lift doors, and there's no-one around but me.
- And I get into the lift, and it goes up, and the doors open, and I'm still on the platform.
- Like that.
- I feel like I'm trapped in this dream and have to work hard to wake myself up and get out of it.

## My dad at the window

- Another dream I had which might be relevant is when I had asthma when I was probably about six or seven, I was always ill with chest infections, and they gave me these asthma pills - Ventolin I think - which made my heart race so fast, and I felt very unwell from them even though they stopped the wheezing.
- I used to tell mum and dad I was "going fast", cos that's how it felt. I guess I could have been hyperventilating.
- One night, when this was happening and I was in bed, I saw dad at the window, and he was sort of standing to one side looking out from behind the curtain so no-one could seem him looking out.
- But he wasn't there actually, and it wasn't my dad. 
- I told them about that too, the man at the window.
- I think that's all except for the lifts... when I was a baby I was terrified of lifts. I wouldn't go in them, I had massive like how do you say, I just went ballistic with fear and terror and they had to take me up the stairs instead.
- I told a lot of people about all this, I hope I don't offend anyone by telling these things, and I'm not saying anything specific either.
- Perhaps when something extraordinarily evil has happened, sensitive people can pick up the traces.
- There was one other thing, whenever I was scared at night in bed - and I was terrified a lot, and often at night going to sleep, I was in general a terrified and anxious child which is interesting given my inner strength, I always thought it was to do with mum because she's so volatile - anyway, whenever I felt like this I used to collect my favorite things in my mind and put them on top of each other and they'd become these big towers; my teddies, my dolls, flowers always flowers, old furniture, books, games.. and I could see towers just like mine all over the mind scape, sort of wobbling.. I might get ChatGPT to do a picture of that one day...
